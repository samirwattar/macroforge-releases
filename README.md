# MacroForge 🔥

**Forge your fuel.** A calorie and macro tracker for Android, made for family and friends.

## Install

1. Open the **[latest release](https://github.com/samirwattar/macroforge-releases/releases/latest)** on your phone and tap the `MacroForge-v….apk` file.
2. When Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser, then tap **Install**.
3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

Android 8.0 or newer. After that, the app tells you itself when a new version is out.

## Premium

Barcode scanning (and more coming) unlocks with a personal code. Ask Samir for yours, then paste it in **Me → Unlock Premium**.

## Privacy

No account and no ads. Everything you log stays on your phone. When you scan a barcode, only that number is sent to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) to look the product up.
